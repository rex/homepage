## What happened

On a Saturday, the venue's virtual SAN failed over, and afterwards the venue's RabbitMQ
couldn't start. Paging didn't break through my phone's silent mode. There were 54 missed
calls, and for about two hours nobody could reach me.

## How I made it worse

To rebuild the broker, I removed the venue from Rancher's multi-cluster RabbitMQ app and
added it back. Rancher's upgrade form defaults the chart version to *latest*, and I didn't
change it. That deployed a much newer Bitnami chart that we had never tested. It failed with
`load_definition.json` not found, and with a secret that didn't contain the key
`rabbitmq-password`.

{/* CONFIRM (Pierce): one source says the "secret does not contain key" error showed up at every venue, which would mean the upgrade reached the whole multi-cluster app. The other says the config matched the healthy venues. Settle whether other venues were touched. */}

## The turn

At 5 p.m. I compared the broken venue's config with the healthy venues', and it was
identical. If the config was the same, what differed had to be the version. I pinned the
chart back to the previous version, and at 5:07 p.m. everything went green at once.

## What changed

- **Paging:** I fixed my phone's bypass settings and reworked how pages reach me.
- **Automated health checks** on the message broker, so a broken broker pages someone instead
  of waiting to be noticed.
- **A runbook** for rebuilding the broker.
- **Explicit chart versions:** I always pin the chart version on Rancher app edits, and never
  accept the form's default.

## What I'd tell someone else

When the config matches a working system, compare versions. A default in a UI is a change
you didn't mean to make.
